    The overall with each stat pair far apart

    From: worklog 0.0.1y's y4

    The overall on the Gameplan pitch, which the drag rule also uses to pick a dragged player's new position, is our reading of PES 2016's formula, and two parts of it are unchecked on PES 2017. Kicking Power's and Swerve's weights may be the wrong way round, and the weight we give Body Control may be Physical Contact's: neither alternative changed any overall read so far. A B position's bonus has never been measured. Four players with each pair far apart, two of them at B positions, settle both.

    1. Copy your PES 2017 save somewhere safe: the check edits it.
    2. Open it in the build, pick a team and click Gameplan. Choose four outfield starters at four different positions (a CB, a CMF, an AMF and a CF, say) and write down their names, in order.
    3. On the Stats screen, give players 1 and 2 Kicking Power 99, Swerve 40, Body Control 40 and Physical Contact 99, and players 3 and 4 the reverse: 40, 99, 99 and 40.
    4. Still on the Stats screen, set each one's positions, Registered position first:
      • player 1: Registered position is the one he plays on the pitch;
      • players 2 and 4: Registered position is another one, and under Playable positions the one he plays on the pitch is B;
      • player 3: the same, with A in place of B.
    5. Back on Gameplan, write down each one's overall on the pitch.
    6. Save, start PES 2017, load the save and open the same team's Game Plan. Write down the four overalls the game shows.
    7. Put back the copy from step 1.
