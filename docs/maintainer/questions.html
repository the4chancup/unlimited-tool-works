    Stats screen: the Playable positions grid 2.5 points higher than the draft's

    From: worklog 0.0.2-gaps, the Stats screen's look after its fix

    On the Save editor's Stats screen, every heading and first row now stands within a point of the draft. The positions grid (the position names and their C, B, A buttons) stands 2.5 points higher than in the draft:

    • 1.5 of it is the grid itself, which stands that much nearer the panel's heading than the draft's;
    • 1 is the whole screen, which stands a point higher than the draft, because the line under the title row is half a point higher and the text snaps to whole pixels.

    The lead let both through as within its tolerance; nobody chose them.

    In the Report a problem dialog, the rows and the text box now have the draft's sizes. The lower half still starts higher: "The file holds:" stands 8 points nearer the title than in the draft. Three things add up to it:
