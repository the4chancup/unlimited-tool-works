    Unscheduled tools

    What the cup's video makers export their swipes as

    From: docs/plans/core/development_plan.md, the Video encoder

    The Video encoder will turn a member's video into the USM the game plays. It reads a PNG image sequence in any case, so someone with no video editor can make a simple swipe; it should also read what the swipe makers export. The 2015 batch files took an AVI and read its transparency from the same file, but what they export today is not known.

    What do the cup's video makers export their swipes and wipes as?
