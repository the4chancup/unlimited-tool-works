    Unscheduled tools

    What the cup's video makers export their swipes as

    From: docs/plans/core/development_plan.md, the Video encoder

    The Video encoder will turn a member's video into the USM the game plays. It reads a PNG image sequence in any case, so someone with no video editor can make a simple swipe; it should also read what the swipe makers export. The 2015 batch files took an AVI and read its transparency from the same file, but what they export today is not known.

    What do the cup's video makers export their swipes and wipes as?

    The PES 2014 build

    How the Akko organizer brings teams over from the 2014 save

    From: the pes14 branch, every team shown on a PES 2014 save

    The PES 2014 build will list every team of a PES 2014 save, so the 2014 cup's teams, which sit at real clubs' IDs (/gd/ at 136, /hm/ at 1236), can be opened and read. Nothing in the editor copies a team from one save into another on PES 2014: that build leaves Team TOML export and import out, and the old save-to-save transplant was never kept.

    How will the organizer bring those teams into the Akko save?
