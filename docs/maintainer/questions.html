    Before the release

    Which game's nations the Nation dropdown lists

    From: worklog 0.0.2-geninfo

    The dropdown lists each version's nations as the base game's own database names them. The 4cc patch ships its own nation table, and it differs on three versions; from PES 2018 on the two are the same.

    • PES 2015: the patch renames 312 SOUTH SUDAN to EQUESTRIA.
    • PES 2016: the patch adds 43 EAST TIMOR, 90 TANZANIA, 130 SAINT KITTS AND NEVIS, 184 PALAU, 249 OFFSIDE and 251 EQUESTRIA.
    • PES 2017: the patch has the PES 2016 patch's list (197 nations against the base game's 220): 25 nations fewer (BHUTAN, MONGOLIA, NEPAL and others), plus OFFSIDE and EQUESTRIA; it also spells 54 and 63 THE COMOROS and REPUBLIC OF THE GAMBIA.

    A nation the list lacks still shows, as its ID and a question mark, and stays until another is picked. One of the test saves has such a player: nation 23 (Mongolia) on PES 2015 and 2016, which neither table lists.
