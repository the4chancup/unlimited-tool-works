    The Compiler's detail window

    A pinned cell scrolled under the title row has its pin covered

    From: DECISIONS.md 2026-11-22 "what the top box shows, and how"; plans/team_compiler/gui.md "The detail window", "A click pins it"

    When the grid is scrolled so a pinned cell sits under the title row, the title row covers the cell's pin as it covers the cell. A pin on the first row is still drawn whole, although it reaches 5 points above its cell. The draft draws a covered cell's pin over the titles, but only because its CSS cannot clip it there.

    Why: a pin drawn over the titles floats where no cell can be seen.

    An archive export's window shows no pictures, for now

    From: DECISIONS.md 2026-11-22 "what the top box shows, and how"; plans/team_compiler/gui.md "The detail window"

    For an export packed as a .zip or .7z, the box at the top of the window stays empty: no logo, no portraits, no kit texture. This lasts until the check keeps a cache of what it read. The draft shows the archive's logo.

    Why: reading one picture out of a .7z means decompressing the whole archive, which the quick check of an archive is there to avoid.

    An export the check skips whole still shows its pictures

    From: DECISIONS.md 2026-11-22 "what the top box shows, and how"; plans/team_compiler/gui.md "The detail window"

    When the check skips a whole export (its team is not in the teams list, say), its cells still open a window, and the box shows the logo, portraits and kit textures the export's files hold. The draft's skipped sample, /egg/, has no logo, so the draft never shows this.

    Why: the window of an export with a problem is the one a member most needs.
