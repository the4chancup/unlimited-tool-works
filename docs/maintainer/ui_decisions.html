    The Compiler's grid

    A kit's menu icon pattern is stretched over the strip at its cell's foot

    From: DECISIONS.md 2026-11-23 "a kit's menu icon pattern is a strip along its cell's foot, stretched to fit"; plans/team_compiler/gui.md "GUI: the live-validating team grid"

    Each kit cell now draws its kit's menu icon pattern (the icon_<N> marker, 3 without one) in the kit's two menu colours, in the strip where the draft draws the two colours as plain halves. The pattern is stretched to the strip's 22 by 9 points, so a sash runs corner to corner and a V spans the whole width. The other way was to draw the pattern in a 9-point square in the strip's middle.

    Why: a square that small would show a sash or a V as a few pixels, and leave most of the strip in one colour.
