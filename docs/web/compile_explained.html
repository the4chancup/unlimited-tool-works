From exports to the game

Once the managers' exports are in the UTW format (the upgrade page shows how they get there), one double-click compiles them. This page shows what the compile reads, what it writes, and what it does when something in an export is wrong.

In one sentence: quick_compile.bat reads every export in exports, builds the CPKs and puts them into the game, then writes the players' looks into the save. The exports are never changed.

What goes in and what comes out

Each box on the left is part of one export; the lines show where it ends up. Point at a box to follow only its lines. The CPK names are those of the league's first DLC, compiled with multicpk_mode = true, a setting explained in "The first DLC and the matchdays after it" below.

What happens, in order

  • Stage 1 can stop the run. When the window says dpfilelist_outdated, the game's DpFileList.bin does not list the league's CPKs yet and nothing was compiled. Step 4 of "Compiling" in README.txt has the command that installs the official list; run quick_compile.bat again after it.
  • Stage 5 needs PES closed. The game writes its own copy of the save over the file when you leave Edit mode, which would undo the patch. With PES running, the compile skips the save and says so (savefile_skipped_pes_running).
  • Which save is patched. The one savefile_path names in data\settings.toml, or the game's own save under Documents\KONAMI when that line is not set. For an official save kept elsewhere, step 3 of "For VGL's organizer" in README.txt has the one command that applies the patch to it.

When something in an export is wrong

The compile never stops at the first problem. It leaves out the smallest part that cannot be built, names it in the window, and builds everything else. The whole export is refused only when the compile cannot tell what it is.

WhereWhat is left outWhat still compiles
A player's folderThat player's folder, for example when a model cannot be read or a texture has a size the game cannot load.The other players, the kits, everything else of the team.
A kit's folderThat kit.The team's other kits and all its players.
A file in Common/That file, and each player whose folder links to it.The rest of Common/ and the other players.
A portraitThat portrait.The player himself, and everything else.
The export itselfThe whole export: a team that teams_list.txt does not hold (team_name_unknown), or two exports of one team in exports (duplicate_aesthetics_export, both refused).The other teams' exports.

Each line in the window names a code, such as texture_not_pow2. The help (the ? button at the bottom of the left bar in UTW.exe) explains every one, and says what the manager has to change. A team with something left out is still installed with the rest: send the manager the lines about their export, and compile again when the fixed export is in.

The first DLC and the matchdays after it

Two settings in data\settings.toml decide which CPKs a compile writes. Steps 1 and 4 of "For VGL's organizer" in README.txt say when to change them.

The first DLCA later matchday
Settingsmulticpk_mode = true multicpk_mode = false and cpk_name = "4cc_61_midcup" (up to 4cc_75_midcup)
What exports holdsEvery team's Full export. Only this matchday's Midcup exports.
CPKs writtenThe teams fill 4cc_41_teams.cpk and the CPKs after it, each up to its size limit. The files every team shares go into 4cc_08_bins.cpk.One CPK, the matchday's own, holding the new files and the shared files with the additions in them.
What it does to a teamA Full export is the team's whole set: a kit it does not hold is removed from the team.A Midcup export only adds: what the team already has stays.

The game loads the CPKs in the order its DpFileList.bin lists them, and a later one covers an earlier one. That is why a matchday's CPK needs only what changed. It is also why the first DLC must not land in 4cc_99_test.cpk, where a compile goes when both settings are left as they come: the game loads that CPK last, so it would cover every matchday after it.

A referee export (its name starts with refs) is compiled with the others and goes into a CPK of its own, 4cc_18_referees.cpk, in either mode.

What the compile window tells you

  • One line per warning or error, then one line that sums up. The last line counts the files written, the exports and folders left out, and the folders with errors.
  • Errors are what to send back to a manager. A line marked Error names something the team needed that was left out. A line marked Warning names something the compile handled by itself, such as a kit that got a fallback texture or a file it had no use for.
  • Every compile writes a new patch. The players' looks live in the save, not in the CPKs. After a later matchday, what goes out to the managers is the new CPK and the newly patched official save together.
  • The patch is the last write to the official save. A .ted imported after it overwrites that team's players, their looks included, so compile again (or apply the patch again) after any import.
